What ARIA actually does

Every leak a busy shop has,
closed automatically.

ARIA runs on the same production autonomous stack as every Belken build, already tuned for the exact workflows service businesses lose the most money on. It is the specialized version of OpenClaw, ready to deploy instead of built from scratch.

Answers every call

After hours, on weekends, and when your lines are already busy. No caller hits a voicemail and dials your competitor instead.

Books the appointment

Qualifies the caller and drops the job straight onto your calendar, so the work is scheduled before the call even ends.

Texts missed callers back

Any caller ARIA cannot reach live gets a text in about a minute with next steps, so the lead stays warm instead of going cold.

Reactivates old customers

Runs outbound campaigns on your dormant customer list and turns idle names into booked service calls.

Requests reviews

Automatically asks for a review after every completed job, so your reputation compounds without anyone remembering to send the text.

Keeps your number and your team

You keep your existing phone number. Your team keeps doing what they do best. ARIA just stops the leaks.
