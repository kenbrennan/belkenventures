What a fleet actually is

Real roles. Real jobs.
No magic.

A fleet is a set of governed AI agents, each with a defined job, wired into the tools your business already runs. Here is the same lineup we run ourselves.

Content operator

Produces and packages work every day. Scripts, drafts, images, and posts, built to your brief and staged for your approval before anything goes out the door.

Research analyst

Watches your market, your competitors, and your inbox for what changed, and hands you the short version every morning.

Operations agent

Runs the QA and reporting loops. Checks the work, catches what broke, and writes the daily summary you actually read.

Engineering agent

Ships against written specs. Builds features, fixes, and internal tools, with every change logged and open to review.

Voice arm

When the business needs phones, a voice agent answers calls, books jobs, and texts people back. This is where ARIA fits, as one part of the fleet.

Every agent is governed, logged, and auditable. Every decision it makes is written down. It runs on infrastructure you control, built on OpenClaw, which is open source, so you own the whole thing and nothing is ever held hostage.
