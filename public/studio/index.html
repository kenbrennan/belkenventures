// Questions

Straight answers.

Do I need a camera or any editing software?
No. Studio runs in your web browser. You paste a script, pick an avatar, and hit Generate. There is no camera to set up and no editing timeline to learn.
Is the person on screen real?
No. The presenter is an AI avatar, and the video is labeled as made with an AI avatar. You choose which avatar delivers your script, and it stays the same across your videos.
Can I post straight to TikTok or YouTube?
Yes. You can download the finished video, or connect a YouTube or TikTok account and publish from Studio. The connection is used to post for you, nothing more.
Are the captions automatic?
Yes. Captions are burned into the video from your script, so it reads clearly with the sound off, which is how most short-form gets watched.
How do I get started?
Create an account at studio.belken.ai and start. Paste a script, pick an avatar, and generate your first video.
