Which one fits you

Choose Belken AI Receptionist if

You want the phone answered, not just texted back

  • You want every inbound call picked up live by an AI that can qualify the caller, answer common questions, and book an appointment — not a voicemail and a text-back.
  • You want outbound dead-list reactivation included in the same flat retainer — calls to past customers who stopped coming back.

  • Predictable flat-rate pricing fits better than a per-message or per-contact usage model.

You want reviews, messaging, and text-back

  • You want to systematically collect Google reviews via automated text after each appointment or job.
  • You want a webchat widget on your website that routes to SMS so you can respond from your phone.
  • You want a centralized inbox for customer texts, Facebook messages, and Google messages in one place.
  • You want automated text-back for missed calls as part of a broader messaging platform — not a live call answering service.

// Questions

Straight answers

What is the main difference between the Belken AI Receptionist and Podium?
Podium is a business messaging and review platform. Its missed call text-back sends an automated text message after a call goes unanswered — but the phone still rings through to voicemail. The Belken AI Receptionist actually picks up the call live, qualifies the lead, and books the appointment. Podium is a broad communications platform for customer reviews and messaging; Belken is a dedicated AI phone receptionist focused entirely on answering calls and recovering dead leads.
Does Podium answer the phone?
Podium's missed call text-back feature sends an automated text after a missed call — the phone goes to voicemail first. Podium does not have an AI voice agent that answers inbound calls, talks to the caller, and books appointments. The Belken AI Receptionist does that: it picks up the phone, speaks to the caller, qualifies them, and books the appointment or routes the call.
Does Belken handle review collection like Podium?
Review collection is not a Belken AI Receptionist feature. Podium is purpose-built for Google review requests, reputation management, and webchat. If review generation and business messaging across multiple channels are the priority, Podium covers that. The Belken AI Receptionist focuses on inbound call answering and outbound dead-list reactivation.
Does the Belken AI Receptionist do outbound calling?
Yes. The Belken AI Receptionist includes dead-list reactivation: outbound calls to past customers who have not come back. That outbound work runs alongside inbound call answering in the same flat retainer.
Which should I choose?
If you want to collect Google reviews, manage customer messaging across channels (text, webchat, social), and send automated texts after missed calls, Podium is built for those things. If you want every inbound call answered live by an AI that can qualify leads, book appointments, and also run outbound reactivation calls to past customers — with no configuration work on your end — that is what the Belken AI Receptionist is for.
