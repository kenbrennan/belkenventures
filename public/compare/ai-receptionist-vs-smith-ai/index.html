Which one fits you

Choose Belken if

You want it run for you

  • You want it fully operated, wired into your calendar, CRM, and phone, with a flat monthly retainer and no per-call meter.
  • You want outbound rebooking of past customers and estimate follow-up built in, not just inbound answering.
  • You want the receptionist as part of a broader operations system Belken runs and reports on.
  • You are a local service business, especially in the trades.

Choose Smith.ai if

You want people, or pay per call

  • You want real people answering your calls, North America-based, 24/7.

  • You want to start quickly without a retainer.

// Questions

Straight answers

What is the main difference between the Belken AI Receptionist and Smith.ai?
Belken sets up and operates an AI receptionist for you as part of a monthly retainer. Smith.ai is a service you buy by call volume: real-people receptionists on its human-first plans, or an AI receptionist on its AI-first plans, which you can set up yourself or, on its $500 per month Enterprise plan, have Smith.ai build and run for you. Belken's receptionist is always set up and operated by Belken, as part of a wider operations retainer.
How is each one priced?
Belken includes the AI Receptionist in its Autonomous Operations Retainer, starting at $4,500 per month with no per-minute meter. Smith.ai bills by the call. Its human-first plans start at 30 calls for $300 per month, and its AI-first plans start free, then $150 per month, and $500 per month for a full-service setup Smith.ai builds and runs. Prices are as shown on smith.ai on 2026-10-11, and prices change.
Does a human ever answer the call?
On Smith.ai's human-first plans, trained people answer every call, and its AI-first plans can escalate to live North America-based agents. The Belken receptionist is an AI that answers in your business's own name and transfers urgent calls to your or your on-call tech's cell phone.
Can I keep my existing phone number?
With Belken, yes, the business keeps its existing phone number and Belken wires the receptionist into the tools you already use. Smith.ai gives you a dedicated local or toll-free number, or lets you port your existing number at no charge.
Does either call my past customers back?
The Belken receptionist calls past customers by name to rebook them, follows up on open estimates, and requests reviews, as part of the operated system. Smith.ai's receptionist plans center on answering and qualifying your inbound calls.
Which should I choose?
If you want a done-for-you AI receptionist that Belken wires into your calendar, CRM, and phone and runs for you at a flat retainer, choose Belken. If you want real people answering, or an AI plan priced by call volume that you can start yourself or have Smith.ai run, choose Smith.ai.
