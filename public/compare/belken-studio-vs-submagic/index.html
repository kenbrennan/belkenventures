// The honest call

Which one fits you

Choose Belken Studio if

You are starting from a script, not footage

  • You do not have footage to work from and want a short made from a script, with a consistent talking AI avatar.
  • You want script-to-video, clipping, and publishing to your own accounts in one place.
  • You want one locked avatar so your whole feed looks like the same creator.
  • You would rather not be on camera at all.

Choose Submagic if

You already have footage and want better captions

  • You record yourself or run a podcast and want animated captions and effects added to your existing clips.
  • Stylized captions, auto-zoom, and visual effects are the primary thing you need.
  • The real person in your footage is the point, so you do not want an AI avatar on screen.
  • You want a self-serve caption tool with a published price and a free tier.

// Questions

Straight answers

What is the main difference between Belken Studio and Submagic?
Submagic takes videos you already have and adds animated captions, auto-zoom, emoji overlays, and other effects on top. Belken Studio creates the video from a script you paste: it renders a talking AI avatar, burns in the captions, and outputs a short ready to post. If you have footage and want to dress it up with captions and effects, Submagic is built for that. If you want to start from a script with no footage at all, Belken Studio makes the video for you.
Does Submagic generate videos from a script?
Submagic is a caption and editing tool. Its core workflow starts from footage you provide, and it has also added AI avatars for making shorts without filming. Belken Studio renders an AI avatar that speaks your script word for word and burns in the captions automatically.
Does Belken Studio add captions to videos I already have?
Belken Studio burns captions in from your script when it generates the avatar video. It can also clip your own long footage into shorts. If your primary need is adding stylized animated captions to an existing video, Submagic is purpose-built for that use case.
How much does each cost?
Submagic publishes plans on its website; check submagic.co for current pricing. Belken Studio does not publish a price on its public page. Pricing is shown in the app when you create an account at studio.belken.ai.
Which should I choose?
If you already have footage and want to make it pop with animated captions and effects, choose Submagic. If you want to turn a script into a short-form video with a consistent AI avatar, no camera, no footage needed, choose Belken Studio.
