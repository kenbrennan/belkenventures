// In plain English

Same technology. Very different goals.

Both can generate a video of an AI presenter speaking from a script. After that, the use cases diverge sharply.

Synthesia is a professional enterprise video platform. It lets you create AI presenter videos for employee onboarding, compliance training, product education, sales enablement, and corporate communications. Its 240+ stock avatars span many appearances, its 140+ language translation works with lip-sync AI, and its SCORM export means the videos plug directly into your LMS. The free Basic tier gives you 10 minutes of video per month, watermarked.

Belken Studio is narrower by design. It turns a script into a short-form social video with a consistent AI avatar, captions burned in, and vertical formatting for TikTok, Reels, and YouTube Shorts. It also clips your own long videos and extracts the best segments automatically. Then it posts directly to your connected TikTok or YouTube account. Everything stays in one workflow: write, generate, clip, publish.

If you produce training content, need to localize video into multiple languages, or work in enterprise L&D, Synthesia is built for that. If you want to build a consistent social presence with short-form video without ever going on camera, and without bouncing between a creator tool, a clipper, and a scheduler, that is what Belken Studio does.

// The honest call

Which one fits you

Choose Belken Studio if

You want short-form social video without switching tools

  • Your goal is TikTok, YouTube Shorts, or Reels, and you want to go from script to posted in one place.
  • You want to clip long footage into short segments and publish them alongside your avatar videos, all in the same tool.
  • You want one locked avatar so your entire social feed looks like the same consistent creator over months, not a rotation of different faces.
  • You would rather not be on camera and want a managed setup that handles generation and publishing in one workflow.

Choose Synthesia if

You produce enterprise video at scale

  • You make training videos, onboarding content, or L&D material that needs to go into an LMS via SCORM export.
  • You need to localize video into multiple languages using lip-sync AI translation, a headline Synthesia capability.
  • You want a wide catalog of avatar options (240+) or the ability to clone your own likeness as a Personal Avatar.
  • You want a free tier to test the product before committing to a paid plan, with no credit card required.

// Questions

Straight answers

What is the main difference between Belken Studio and Synthesia?
Synthesia is an enterprise AI video platform built for training, learning and development, HR onboarding, and professional presentations. It offers 240-plus stock avatars, translation into 140-plus languages with lip-sync AI on its Enterprise plan, and SCORM export for LMS integration. Belken Studio is built for short-form social video: paste a script and it renders a vertical short with a consistent AI avatar and burned-in captions, ready to publish to TikTok or YouTube Shorts. It also clips your own long videos into shorts. If you need enterprise training content, language localization, or LMS compatibility, Synthesia is built for that. If you want to create and publish short-form social video without being on camera, Belken Studio is built for that workflow.
Does Synthesia clip long videos into short clips for social media?
Not as a listed feature on synthesia.io as of 2026-10-17. Synthesia is designed to create new AI presenter videos from a script, translate existing videos into other languages, and produce professional widescreen content for enterprise distribution. It is not primarily a tool for clipping and reformatting footage into vertical social shorts. Belken Studio includes a built-in long-video clipper that extracts short clips with burned-in captions formatted for TikTok and YouTube Shorts.
Can both create AI talking head videos from a script?
Yes. Both tools generate a video of an AI avatar speaking text you provide. Synthesia gives you a catalog of 240-plus stock avatars on Enterprise, and on Pro and Enterprise plans you can create a Personal Avatar from your own likeness. Belken Studio uses one locked avatar per account, which means your entire social feed looks like the same consistent creator over time rather than cycling through different faces or avatars.
Does Belken Studio support video translation like Synthesia?
Belken Studio's public pages do not list video translation or dubbing as a feature as of 2026-10-17. Synthesia offers 1-click translation into 140-plus languages on its Enterprise plan, allowing you to take an AI video and produce a localized version automatically with lip-sync matching. If reaching audiences in multiple languages is your primary goal, Synthesia is purpose-built for that use case.
How much does each one cost?
Synthesia publishes its pricing: Basic at $0 per month (500 credits, roughly 10 minutes of video, watermarked, share link only), Starter at $29 per month (1,250 credits, roughly 12 minutes of video, 125-plus avatars, no watermark, downloadable), Pro at $89 per month (6,000 credits, roughly 60 minutes of video, 5 Personal Avatars, 180-plus avatars), and Enterprise at custom pricing (unlimited video, 240-plus avatars, 140-plus language translation, SCORM export, SAML/SSO). All figures from synthesia.io/pricing, checked 2026-10-17. Prices change. Belken Studio does not publish a price on its public pages. Pricing is shown in the app when you create an account at studio.belken.ai.
Which should I choose?
If you produce training videos, e-learning content, HR communications, or need to localize video into many languages with lip-sync AI, Synthesia is purpose-built for those use cases. If you want to build a short-form social presence, go from script to posted video in one place, clip long footage into shorts, and maintain one consistent AI avatar across your feed, Belken Studio is designed for that workflow. The simplest test: if your output is going into an LMS or a corporate portal, use Synthesia. If it is going onto TikTok or YouTube Shorts, try Belken Studio.
