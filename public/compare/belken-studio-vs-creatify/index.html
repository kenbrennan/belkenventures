// In plain English

Paid ads vs organic content: different goals

Both tools produce short-form video with AI avatars. They are built for different destinations and different strategies.

Creatify describes itself as the number one AI ad platform built for performance. The workflow starts with a product page URL or ad brief, and it generates video ad variations optimized for Meta, TikTok, YouTube, Snap, and Amazon ad accounts. It supports batch creating dozens of variations at once, built-in A/B testing across campaigns, and analytics that show spend, ROAS, and CTR by creative. It is trusted by 18,000 brands and agencies and has generated over 15 million ads.

Belken Studio is not an ad platform. It is built for organic short-form social content. You paste a script about your business or import a long video (a podcast, a webinar, a screen recording) and it outputs a vertical short with an AI avatar presenting the content, captions burned in, and formatting ready for TikTok Shorts, YouTube Shorts, or Reels. It then posts directly to your connected creator accounts. The same locked AI avatar appears in every video, which builds a consistent creator identity across your feed over time.

If your goal is paid advertising at scale with ad-account integration and ROAS tracking, Creatify is purpose-built for that. If your goal is organic social presence using an AI avatar for content that looks like it comes from a real creator, Belken Studio is built for that. The tools can complement each other: organic content in Studio, paid campaigns in Creatify.

// The honest call

Which one fits you

Choose Belken Studio if

You want organic social presence with a consistent AI avatar

  • Your goal is to build an organic TikTok or YouTube channel with short-form content without being on camera.
  • You have a script about your business, or long video you want clipped into shorts, and you want both in one place.
  • You want one locked avatar so your whole social feed looks like the same consistent creator over months and years.
  • You want to post to your organic creator accounts, not to an ad campaign manager.

Choose Creatify if

You are running paid video ad campaigns at scale

  • You need to turn a product URL into dozens of video ad variations and launch them to Meta or TikTok Ads Manager.
  • You want A/B testing across ad creatives with ROAS and CTR analytics to see what actually converts.
  • You need variety across ad actors rather than one consistent creator identity, to test different hooks and angles.
  • You are a brand or agency managing paid ad spend and need enterprise-grade security and batch production.

// Questions

Straight answers

What is the main difference between Belken Studio and Creatify?
Creatify is a performance advertising platform built for brands and agencies running paid ad campaigns. It takes a product URL or ad brief and generates video ads optimized for Meta, TikTok, YouTube, and Snap ad accounts, with A/B testing and ROAS analytics built in. Belken Studio is built for organic short-form social content: paste a script or import a long video, and it produces a short with a consistent AI avatar and captions, then publishes it to your TikTok creator or YouTube account. Creatify is about paid ad performance at scale. Belken Studio is about organic social presence without being on camera.
Can Creatify post to my social media creator accounts?
Creatify connects to ad accounts on Meta, TikTok, YouTube, Snap, and Amazon for launching paid campaigns. It is designed for the advertising workflow, not the organic creator workflow. Belken Studio connects to your TikTok and YouTube creator accounts and posts organic content directly to your feed after you approve each video.
Does Creatify use a consistent AI avatar like Belken Studio?
Creatify offers access to 300 AI actors on its free plan and a larger library on paid plans. You can pick different actors for different ads, which is useful when you want variety across campaigns and want to test which avatar style converts better. Belken Studio uses one locked AI avatar per account. Every video that comes out of Belken Studio looks like the same person, which builds a consistent creator identity across your social feed over time.
Which is better for a service business that wants social media presence without running ads?
Belken Studio is built for that use case. It is not an ad platform. You paste a script about your service, it renders a short with your AI avatar and captions, and you post it to your organic TikTok or YouTube channel. If you also want to run paid video ads alongside your organic content, Creatify handles the paid side. The two tools are not direct substitutes; a business doing both organic content and paid ads could use both.
How much does each one cost?
Creatify has a free plan with 10 monthly credits (up to 2 video ads or 20 image ads, 300 AI actors, watermark on exports). Paid plan pricing was not available on the public page without JavaScript as of 2026-10-18; check creatify.ai/pricing for current rates. Belken Studio does not publish pricing on its public pages. Pricing is shown in the app after you create an account at studio.belken.ai.
Which tool should I use for AI avatar video on TikTok?
It depends on whether you mean paid TikTok ads or organic TikTok content. If you want to run paid video ad campaigns through TikTok Ads Manager, track ROAS, and batch-create ad variations, Creatify is built for that. If you want to publish short-form organic video to your TikTok creator account using a consistent AI avatar and build a following without running ads, Belken Studio is built for that. Both produce AI avatar video; the workflow and destination are different.
