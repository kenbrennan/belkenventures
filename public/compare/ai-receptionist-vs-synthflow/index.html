Belken is a fully managed service: Belken sets up the AI receptionist, wires it into your existing phone system, calendar, and CRM, and continues to tune and monitor it on a flat retainer. The trade-off is build-it-yourself flexibility versus having it handled for you.

// The honest call

Which one fits you

Choose Belken AI Receptionist if

You want it handled, not built

  • You run a trades, home services, or local service business and do not want to configure or manage a voice AI system yourself.
  • You want inbound answering and outbound dead-list reactivation in the same flat retainer, with zero configuration on your end.
  • You want Belken to wire the AI into your calendar, CRM, and existing phone number, and keep it tuned as things change.
  • A flat monthly retainer with predictable pricing fits your budget better than a usage-based model.

Choose Synthflow if

You want to build and run it yourself

  • You or someone on your team has the technical capacity to build, configure, and maintain custom AI voice agents.
  • You want direct control over the call flow logic, scripts, and integrations.
  • You are an agency or developer building AI phone solutions for multiple clients.
  • You prefer a self-serve product where you own the platform configuration entirely.

// Questions

Straight answers

What is the main difference between the Belken AI Receptionist and Synthflow?
Synthflow is a self-serve platform: you build AI voice agents using their no-code builder, configure the call flows, connect integrations, and run the system yourself. Belken is a fully managed service: Belken sets up the AI receptionist, wires it into your existing phone system, calendar, and CRM, and continues to tune and monitor it on a flat retainer. If you want to build and run a custom voice agent yourself, Synthflow is the self-serve route. If you want it handled for you with no configuration work on your end, that is the Belken AI Receptionist.
Does Synthflow work for inbound and outbound calls?
Synthflow supports both inbound and outbound AI voice calling. You configure the AI agents for whichever use case you need using their platform. Belken also handles both inbound answering and outbound dead-list reactivation calls, but the difference is Belken manages the entire operation as a service, you do not configure anything.
Does Belken do outbound reactivation, not just inbound answering?
Yes. The Belken AI Receptionist includes dead-list reactivation: outbound calls to past customers who have not come back. That outbound reactivation runs alongside inbound call answering in the same flat retainer. You do not need to configure it separately.
How does pricing compare?
Synthflow publishes its plans on synthflow.ai; check there for current pricing. Belken operates as a managed retainer: contact Belken at belken.ai/aria for pricing, as it is set based on your call volume and business setup.
Which should I choose?
If you have technical capacity to build, configure, and run AI voice agents yourself, or if you want full control over the call flow logic, Synthflow is built for that. If you want someone to set up and run the AI receptionist for your trades, home services, or local service business without any configuration work on your end, that is what the Belken AI Receptionist is for.
