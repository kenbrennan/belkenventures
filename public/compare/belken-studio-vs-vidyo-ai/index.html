// The key difference

Both clip long videos. One posts directly; the other makes AI avatar ads.

Quso.ai (formerly Vidyo.ai) is a pure clipping and social publishing machine with direct posting to 7 platforms. Belken Studio clips long video and uniquely generates AI talking-head avatar ads from a script.

Quso.ai (formerly Vidyo.ai) is one of the most established AI video clipping tools. You upload a long-form video -- a podcast, YouTube video, webinar, stream, or interview -- and it finds the best moments, adds captions, and formats them for TikTok, Reels, and YouTube Shorts. The credit model means one credit is roughly one minute of processed video; 75 credits per month on the free tier, scaling to 600 credits per month on the Growth plan. On the Essential plan ($39/mo) and above, you can schedule and publish directly to 7 platforms including TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, and others. The tool does not generate AI avatar ads. It is a clipping and distribution tool.

Belken Studio is also a video clipping tool, but it adds a second capability Quso.ai does not have: AI avatar ad generation. Paste a script and Belken Studio renders a short-form talking-head video with a consistent locked AI avatar, burned-in captions, and vertical formatting. The free tier ($0/mo, no card needed) includes 60 minutes of source video per month for clipping. The paid entry plan is $19/mo for 10 hours per month. The AI avatar ads feature starts at $49/mo and produces roughly 4 to 6 short ads per month. As of 2026-10-18, direct social publishing is waiting on platform API approvals; you download finished clips and post from your own accounts.

If you want a focused clipping tool with working direct social publishing today and you do not need AI avatar ads, Quso.ai is the more proven choice for that workflow. If your goal is AI talking-head ads from a script without going on camera, or if you want a video-first tool with a clear free tier while you evaluate, Belken Studio covers that job.

// The honest call

Which one fits you

Choose Belken Studio if

You need AI avatar ads, or want a focused clipping tool while direct publishing rolls out

  • You want to generate AI talking-head ads from a script without going on camera -- a consistent AI avatar that looks the same in every ad you produce.
  • You have long-form video to clip and want to start free with no card. The $0 tier gives you 60 minutes of source video per month with downloads.
  • You want an hour-based processing model (10 to 50 hours per month) rather than a credit-per-minute system that can run out mid-batch.
  • You are a creator or business evaluating AI video tools now and are comfortable posting manually while direct social publishing gets API approval.

Choose Quso.ai (Vidyo.ai) if

You want a proven clipping tool with working direct social publishing today

  • You want to upload long video and have it scheduled and posted to TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, and other platforms without any manual posting steps.
  • You want a tool used by 4M+ creators with a track record. Quso.ai (Vidyo.ai) has been in market longer and has broad community support.
  • You want AI filler and silence removal baked in (available on Essential, $39/mo) to clean up raw footage automatically.
  • You do not need AI avatar ad generation and want a focused clip-and-publish workflow at a lower starting price ($29/mo Lite).

// Questions

Straight answers

What is the difference between Belken Studio and Vidyo.ai (Quso.ai)?
Both tools clip long-form videos into short captioned clips for TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Reels. The main differences are: (1) Direct publishing: Quso.ai posts directly to 7 platforms on paid plans today; Belken Studio's direct publishing is waiting on platform API approvals as of 2026-10-18 and you download and post manually in the meantime. (2) AI avatar ads: Belken Studio can generate short talking-head videos from a script using a consistent AI avatar, starting at $49/mo. Quso.ai does not have this feature. (3) Processing model: Quso.ai is credit-based (1 credit is roughly 1 minute of video processed); Belken Studio is hour-based (10 to 50 hours per month on paid plans).
Is Vidyo.ai the same as Quso.ai?
Yes. Vidyo.ai rebranded to Quso.ai. Visiting vidyo.ai redirects to quso.ai. The quso.ai pricing page notes: "Coming from Vidyo.ai? Your plan and pricing are unchanged." The product is the same AI video clipping tool. Quso.ai's own Organization schema on the page lists "alternateName: Vidyo.ai" confirming the rebrand. If you were researching Vidyo.ai features or looking for a Vidyo.ai alternative, the current product is at quso.ai.
Does Belken Studio post directly to TikTok and Instagram?
Not yet. As of 2026-10-18, studio.belken.ai states that direct publishing to TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, and Facebook is waiting on each platform's API approval. Until that clears, you download finished clips from Belken Studio and post them from your own accounts. Quso.ai publishes directly to TikTok on the free plan, and to 7 platforms on the Essential plan ($39/mo) and above.
Does Quso.ai have AI avatar video generation?
No. Quso.ai finds the best moments in your existing footage and clips and publishes them. It does not generate a talking-head AI avatar video from a script. If you need to produce AI avatar ads without appearing on camera yourself, Belken Studio is the relevant tool: paste a script, choose an avatar, and it renders a short-form video with a consistent locked AI avatar and burned-in captions. AI ads start on the $49/mo plan (roughly 4 to 6 short ads per month).
How much does Vidyo.ai (Quso.ai) cost in 2026?
As of 2026-10-18, Quso.ai (formerly Vidyo.ai) has a free tier and three paid plans. Free: $0/mo, 75 credits per month (1 credit is roughly 1 minute of processed video), 720p quality, TikTok publishing, 7-day data retention. Lite: $29/mo ($174/yr), 100 credits/month, 1080p, advanced video editor (desktop), TikTok, 10 GB storage. Essential: $39/mo ($234/yr), 300 credits/month, schedule to 7 platforms, AI filler and silence removal, 25 GB storage. Growth: $49/mo ($294/yr), 600 credits/month, custom Brand Kit and templates, social analytics, priority support, 75 GB storage. Prices from quso.ai/pricing, checked 2026-10-18. Prices change.
How much does Belken Studio cost?
Belken Studio has four plans. Free: $0/mo (no card needed), 60 minutes source video per month, captioned clips, downloads -- no AI avatar generation on the free plan. Clip and post: $19/mo ($180/yr), 10 hours per month source video, unlimited clips, all caption styles, scheduling, up to 3 accounts. Clip, post and make AI ads: $49/mo ($468/yr), 50 hours per month, 10 accounts, analytics, autopilot, 2 generated minutes of AI avatar video per month (about 4 to 6 short ads). Multiple brands: $149/mo ($1,428/yr), 6 generated minutes AI avatar video (12 to 18 ads), flow canvas. Prices from studio.belken.ai, checked 2026-10-18. Prices change.
