// The honest call

Which one fits you

Choose Belken Studio if

You are starting from a script, not footage

  • You do not have long videos to clip and want a short made from just a script, with a consistent AI avatar.
  • You want script-to-video, clipping, and publishing to your own accounts in one place.
  • You want one locked avatar so your whole feed looks like the same creator.
  • You would rather not be on camera at all.

Choose OpusClip if

You already produce long videos

  • You make podcasts, streams, webinars, or long YouTube videos and want to clip them at volume.
  • You need scale features like bulk export, a social scheduler, a team workspace, clip analytics, and an API.
  • You want a published self-serve price and a free tier to start today.
  • The faces in your footage are the point, so you do not want an avatar.

// Questions

Straight answers

What is the main difference between Belken Studio and OpusClip?
OpusClip turns your existing long videos into short clips. Belken Studio turns a script into a short-form video with an AI avatar talking and captions burned in, and it can also clip your own long footage. If you already have footage to cut, OpusClip is built for that at volume. If you are starting from a script with no footage, Belken Studio makes the video for you.
Does OpusClip create a talking avatar from a script?
Not on the plans we checked. The OpusClip pricing page on 2026-10-11 lists features for clipping and editing existing footage, such as AI captions, AI reframe, AI voiceover, and B-roll, and does not list a script-to-avatar feature. Belken Studio renders a talking AI avatar from a script you paste.
Does Belken Studio also clip long videos?
Yes. Belken Studio makes short-form video whether you generate it from a script with an avatar or turn your own long footage into clips, and it can publish the result to your own accounts.
How much does each one cost?
OpusClip publishes plans on its pricing page: Free at $0, Starter at $15 per month, Pro at $29 per month, and Business at a custom price, as shown on 2026-10-11. Prices change, so check its page. Belken Studio does not publish a price on its public page. Pricing is shown in the app when you create an account at studio.belken.ai.
Can both post to my social accounts?
Yes. Belken Studio posts to your YouTube, TikTok, Facebook Page, or Instagram after you connect the account and confirm the post. OpusClip posts to YouTube Shorts, TikTok, and Instagram, with a social scheduler on its higher plans.
Which should I choose?
If you have a library of long videos and want to clip them at scale, choose OpusClip. If you want to turn a script into a short with a consistent AI avatar, and clip and publish to your own accounts in one place, choose Belken Studio.
