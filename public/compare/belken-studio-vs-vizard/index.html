// In plain English

Both clip. Belken Studio also creates and publishes.

Vizard and Belken Studio both turn long videos into short-form clips. The difference is what else each tool does in the same workflow.

Vizard is a focused AI video editor and clipper. You import your long-form content, and it uses AI to find engaging moments, cut them to the right length, center the speaker in a vertical frame, add subtitles, and remove filler words. The idea is to make it fast to turn a podcast, stream, or long recording into several TikTok-ready clips without hand-editing each one.

Belken Studio also clips long videos. But it adds two things Vizard does not. First, a script-to-avatar feature: paste a script and the tool generates a short-form video with a consistent AI avatar speaking it, captions burned in, formatted for TikTok or Reels. You can create content without any existing footage and without ever going on camera. Second, direct social publishing: connect your TikTok or YouTube account and publish from inside the same tool, without exporting to a file and then uploading separately.

If your main task is editing existing footage into clips, Vizard is a tight tool for that. If you want to combine long-video clipping with generating new avatar videos from scratch and publishing directly to your channels, Belken Studio handles all three in one place.

// Questions

Straight answers

What is the main difference between Belken Studio and Vizard?
Both tools clip long videos into shorter segments for social media. The main difference is what else each one does. Vizard is focused on editing and clipping footage you already have: it finds engaging moments, resizes to vertical, adds subtitles, and removes filler words. Belken Studio does that clipping, but it also generates brand-new short-form videos from a script using an AI avatar, so you can create content even without any existing footage. Belken Studio also connects to your social accounts and publishes the finished video directly to TikTok or YouTube, without a separate scheduling tool.
Does Vizard create AI avatar videos from a script?
Not as a listed feature on vizard.ai as of 2026-10-17. Vizard is built around editing footage you bring in. Belken Studio includes an AI avatar feature: you paste a script, the tool renders an avatar speaking it with burned-in captions in vertical format, and you can post it directly to TikTok or YouTube. If you want to create short-form content without existing footage and without going on camera, that is what Belken Studio's avatar feature is for.
Can both clip long videos into short social clips?
Yes. Both tools can take a long video and extract shorter segments for TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Reels. Both add automatic captions or subtitles to the clips. Vizard focuses on this clipping and editing workflow as its core product and has features like filler word removal built around it. Belken Studio includes the same long-video clipping alongside AI avatar video generation and direct social publishing in one place.
Does Belken Studio or Vizard publish directly to TikTok and YouTube?
Belken Studio connects to your TikTok, YouTube, Facebook Page, and Instagram accounts and publishes the video after you confirm it, without needing a separate scheduler. Vizard's direct social publishing capabilities were not confirmed from a live page read on 2026-10-17. Check vizard.ai directly for the current feature set before deciding.
How much does each one cost?
Belken Studio does not publish a price on its public pages. Pricing is shown in the app when you create an account at studio.belken.ai. Vizard's pricing page uses JavaScript and could not be parsed in this review. We did not confirm specific plan prices. Check vizard.ai/pricing for current plans before making a decision.
Which should I choose?
If your primary goal is editing and clipping existing footage, including filler word removal and multi-clip batch extraction from long recordings, Vizard is built around that workflow. If you want to both clip existing footage and create brand-new short-form videos from a script without going on camera, with one consistent AI avatar across your feed, published directly to TikTok or YouTube without a separate tool, Belken Studio handles all three in one place.
