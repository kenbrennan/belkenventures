// The honest call

Which one fits you

Choose Belken Studio if

You are starting from a script, not footage

  • You do not have existing footage and want to make a short from just a script, with a consistent AI avatar.
  • You want the same face in every video so your feed looks like one creator.
  • You want script-to-video, clipping, and publishing to your own accounts in one place.
  • You would rather not be on camera at all.

Choose Vizard if

You already have long videos to repurpose

  • You have a library of podcasts, interviews, or long-form videos you want to turn into clips.
  • You want the AI to find the best moments for you rather than choosing timestamps yourself.
  • You want automatic reframing to keep the speaker centered in vertical format.
  • You want a self-serve clipping tool with a published price.

// Questions

Straight answers

What is the main difference between Belken Studio and Vizard?
Vizard is built to clip your existing long videos into short, engaging clips. It automatically identifies the best moments and reframes them to vertical. Belken Studio creates a video from a script you paste: it renders a talking AI avatar, burns in the captions, and outputs a short ready to post. Vizard needs footage to start. Belken Studio starts from a blank page — just words. Both can produce short-form content for TikTok, Reels, and Shorts, but they solve different problems.
Does Vizard generate video from a script with no footage?
Vizard's core workflow starts from video footage you already have. It clips and edits what you provide. Belken Studio renders a talking AI avatar from a script you paste — no footage, no camera needed.
Does Belken Studio also clip long videos?
Yes. Belken Studio makes short-form video whether you generate it from a script with an avatar or turn your own long footage into clips, and it can publish the videos straight to your own TikTok or YouTube account.
How much does each one cost?
Vizard publishes plans on its website; check vizard.ai/pricing for current pricing. Belken Studio does not publish a price on its public page. Pricing is shown in the app when you create an account at studio.belken.ai.
Which should I choose?
If you have existing long videos — podcasts, interviews, webinars — and want to cut them into short clips at volume, Vizard is built for that. If you want to turn a script into a short-form video with a consistent AI avatar, no camera, no footage needed, Belken Studio makes the video for you.
