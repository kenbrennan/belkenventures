// The honest call

Which one fits you

Choose Belken AI Receptionist if

You want it handled, not configured

  • You run a trades, home services, or local service business and do not want to configure a system yourself.
  • You want inbound answering and outbound reactivation calls in the same retainer.
  • You want Belken to wire it into your calendar, CRM, and phone, and keep it tuned as things change.
  • A flat monthly retainer with no per-minute billing fits your budget better.

Choose Goodcall if

You want to configure and control it yourself

  • You or someone on your team has capacity to build, configure, and maintain the AI phone system.
  • You want a self-serve product with direct access to the configuration tools.
  • Inbound call answering is the primary need, without outbound reactivation campaigns.
  • You prefer a published self-serve plan over a managed retainer arrangement.

// Questions

Straight answers

What is the main difference between the Belken AI Receptionist and Goodcall?
Goodcall is a self-serve product: you sign up, configure the AI yourself using their builder, and manage it on your own. Belken is a fully managed service: Belken sets up the AI receptionist, wires it into your phone, calendar, and CRM, and continues to tune and monitor it for you. If you want to configure and run a system yourself, Goodcall is the self-serve route. If you want it handled for you on a flat monthly retainer, that is what Belken offers.
Does Goodcall integrate with my calendar and CRM?
Goodcall has integrations with common calendar and business tools. You configure those integrations yourself as part of setup. Belken wires those integrations as part of the service and continues to maintain them.
Does Belken do outbound calls, not just inbound?
Yes. The Belken AI Receptionist includes dead-list reactivation: outbound calls to past customers who have not come back. That outbound reactivation is included in the same flat retainer as inbound call answering. Goodcall is focused on inbound call answering.
How does pricing compare?
Goodcall publishes its plans on goodcall.com; check there for current pricing. Belken operates as a managed retainer: contact Belken at belken.ai/aria for pricing, as it is set based on your call volume and setup.
Which should I choose?
If you want to configure and manage an AI phone system yourself at a self-serve price, Goodcall is built for that. If you want someone to set it up, keep it tuned, and run outbound reactivation alongside inbound answering on a flat retainer, with no configuration work on your end, that is the Belken AI Receptionist.
