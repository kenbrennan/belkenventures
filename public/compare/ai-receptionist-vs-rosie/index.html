Which one fits you

Choose Belken AI Receptionist if

You want inbound plus outbound, fully managed

  • You want inbound answering and outbound reactivation calls to lapsed customers in the same service.
  • You do not want to configure or manage a system yourself, Belken handles setup and ongoing tuning.
  • You want the AI wired into your calendar, CRM, and phone without doing the wiring yourself.
  • A flat monthly retainer fits better than per-minute or per-call pricing.

Choose Rosie if

You need inbound coverage and can configure it yourself

  • After-hours and overflow inbound call answering is the primary need.
  • You or someone on your team can configure and manage the call flows yourself.
  • You do not have an immediate need for outbound reactivation campaigns.
  • You prefer a published self-serve price you can start with today.

// Questions

Straight answers

What is the main difference between the Belken AI Receptionist and Rosie?
Rosie focuses on inbound call answering: when your phone rings after hours or when you are busy, Rosie answers, qualifies the caller, and takes a message or books an appointment. Belken does the same for inbound calls and also adds outbound dead-list reactivation, calls to past customers who have not come back, as part of the same flat retainer. Belken is also fully managed: Belken sets up, wires, and continues to tune the system for you.
Does Rosie do outbound calls?
Rosie is built for inbound call answering. Check their site for any outbound features. The Belken AI Receptionist includes outbound dead-list reactivation calls to past customers as part of the same retainer as inbound answering.
How does Belken set up the AI receptionist compared to Rosie?
Belken is a fully managed service. Belken handles the setup, wires the AI into your phone system, calendar, and CRM, and continues to monitor and tune it as your call patterns change. With Rosie, you configure the system yourself using their platform.
How does pricing compare?
Rosie publishes plans on heyrosie.com; check there for current pricing. Belken operates as a managed retainer: contact Belken at belken.ai/aria for pricing based on your call volume and setup.
Which should I choose?
If you primarily need after-hours inbound call answering and want to configure the system yourself, Rosie is worth checking. If you need both inbound answering and outbound reactivation campaigns, and you want someone to set up, operate, and tune the system for you on a flat retainer, that is the Belken AI Receptionist.
