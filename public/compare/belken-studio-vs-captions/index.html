Belken Studio vs Captions AI

// The key difference

Different starting points for different creators

Captions starts with footage you already have. Belken Studio starts with a script and creates the footage for you.

Captions AI is a mobile app (iOS-first) built around the problem of making existing footage look more polished and reach farther. You record a video on your phone, open Captions, and the app adds animated captions in dozens of styles, trims dead air, adjusts zoom, adds AI effects, and can translate the video into another language with lip-sync AI. On paid plans you can also use an AI avatar ("AI Twin") and clip a long video into a batch of shorter pieces. It has a free tier and is designed around a phone-native editing workflow.

Belken Studio runs in a web browser and starts from a different place: you do not need any footage at all. Paste a script and it generates a short-form video with a consistent AI avatar, burned-in captions, and vertical formatting for TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Reels. You can also import a long video and clip it into shorts. The output goes directly to your connected social accounts from inside the same tool, without exporting and re-uploading.

If you are already shooting content on your phone and want to edit and enhance it faster, Captions AI is built for that workflow. If you want to create consistent short-form content without ever being on camera, from a browser, with the posting step built in, that is what Belken Studio is designed for.

// The honest call

Which one fits you

Choose Belken Studio if

You want script-to-published social video without switching tools

  • You do not want to be on camera. Give Belken Studio a script and it generates an AI avatar video, no filming needed.
  • You want one locked avatar so every video on your feed looks like the same consistent creator over time, not a different face each post.
  • You want the publish step built in, not a separate upload. Connect TikTok or YouTube and post from inside Studio without exporting.
  • You prefer working from a desktop web browser, not a phone app.

Choose Captions AI if

You are already filming and want a fast mobile editing app

  • You film footage on your iPhone and want a fast way to add animated captions, effects, and AI edits without switching to a desktop.
  • You want to translate an existing video into another language with lip-sync AI, a feature Captions AI specializes in.
  • You want highly customizable caption styles and animated caption templates, with 100+ options on paid plans.
  • You want a free tier to test before committing, or need AI voice cloning for your own voice.

// Questions

Straight answers

What is the main difference between Belken Studio and Captions AI?
Captions AI is a mobile-first iOS app built around adding AI captions, effects, avatars, and translations to footage you already have. It is strong on caption styles, animated effects, and video lip-dub translation into other languages. Belken Studio is a web-based tool built script-first: you paste a script and it generates a short-form social video with a consistent AI avatar and captions, then posts it to your connected TikTok or YouTube account without you needing to export the file. The fundamental difference is where the workflow starts: Captions starts with footage you already filmed; Belken Studio creates the footage from your script.
Does Captions AI publish directly to TikTok or YouTube?
Captions AI does not list direct social publishing as a primary feature on its pricing or features pages as of 2026-10-17. The typical workflow is to create the video in the app and export it, then upload it to your social accounts manually. Belken Studio includes a publishing step inside the tool: connect your TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, or Facebook account and post each video directly after you confirm it.
Can both tools clip long videos into short clips?
Yes. Captions AI includes a "Clips" feature on its Max ($24.99/mo) and Frontier ($69.99/mo) plans that turns a long video into a batch of post-ready shorter clips. Belken Studio also includes long-video clipping: import a long video and it extracts short segments with burned-in captions formatted for TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Reels, then lets you post them directly from the same interface.
Is Captions AI a mobile app or a web app?
Captions AI is primarily a mobile app. Its pricing page notes that features and prices reflect iOS plans only as of 2026-10-17, which suggests the main product experience is on iPhone. Belken Studio is a web-based tool that runs in a browser on desktop or mobile web and does not require installing a phone app.
How much does Captions AI cost?
Captions AI has a Free plan at $0 with traditional video editing tools (trim, zoom, resize, captions in 100+ languages) but only 1 caption template and no AI generation features. The Max plan is $24.99 per month and includes 500 AI credits per month, access to AI Twin (avatar), AI Edit, AI Ads, the Clips feature, AI Translate, AI Lipdub, AI Voice Clone, and other AI features. The Frontier plan is $69.99 per month with 1,400 credits per month and access to the most sophisticated AI models. Enterprise pricing is custom. As shown on captions.ai/pricing on 2026-10-17. Captions AI notes that prices reflect iOS plans only. Prices change; check their page.
Which should I choose?
If you are already filming content on a phone and want a fast, mobile-first app to add polished captions and effects, need video translation or lip-dub, want voice cloning, or want to start free, choose Captions AI. If you want to create short-form social videos from a script without ever being on camera, want one consistent AI avatar across all your content, need the publish step built into the same tool that creates the video, and prefer working from a web browser, choose Belken Studio.
