Belken Studio vs Munch Studio

Munch Studio manages your brand's entire social content calendar with AI — scripts, videos, carousels, posters, and scheduled publishing across Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube, and TikTok. Belken Studio is a video-only tool: upload long footage and clip it into captioned shorts, or paste a script and generate an AI avatar talking-head ad. // The key difference

A social content manager vs a video production tool

Munch Studio handles your social strategy, calendar, and publishing across all formats. Belken Studio does one thing: make great short-form video.

Munch Studio is a done-for-you social media content platform. You connect your accounts and point it at your website, existing posts, and brand materials. The AI trains on all of that — learning your voice, tone, and topics — then generates a content plan for the week or quarter: video scripts, short-form videos, static carousels, image posters, and a posting calendar. You review the plan, approve or edit, and Munch publishes on schedule to Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube, and TikTok. The Essential plan ($38/mo annually) includes 500 minutes of video repurposing per month, 10 generated videos, and 5 scripts per week. The Premium plan ($60/mo annually) doubles the repurposing and triples the generated videos, and adds unlimited team members.

Belken Studio is a video production tool that does not touch your social calendar or brand strategy. It has two jobs: take a long video (a podcast, webinar, or interview) and clip it into captioned short-form segments formatted for TikTok and YouTube Shorts, or take a script and generate a short talking-head video with a consistent AI avatar. The AI ads plan ($49/mo) produces roughly 4 to 6 short avatar ads per month. There is a free tier at $0/mo (no card needed) for clipping only. As of 2026-10-18, direct publishing to social platforms is waiting on each platform's API approval; you download the finished videos and post them from your own accounts.

If the problem is "I need a consistent social presence but I do not want to manage a content calendar," Munch Studio is built for that. If the problem is "I have hours of long-form video that needs to become short clips" or "I need AI talking-head ads without appearing on camera," Belken Studio is the more focused tool.

// The honest call

Which one fits you

Choose Belken Studio if

You have long-form video or need AI avatar ads without a full social manager

  • You have long-form video (podcasts, webinars, interviews, recorded calls) and want a fast way to clip the best moments into captioned short-form content.
  • You want to generate AI talking-head ads from a script without going on camera, with a consistent avatar that looks the same video after video.
  • You want to start free — Belken Studio has a $0/mo tier with no card required for clipping up to 60 minutes of source video per month.
  • You want a simple, focused video tool without a broader social management subscription layered on top.

Choose Munch Studio if

You want AI to run your brand's full social calendar hands-free

  • You want an AI that learns your brand's voice and tone from your existing content, then generates new posts in your style without you briefing each one from scratch.
  • You need more than video — you also want AI-generated carousels, posters, scripts, and a full weekly or quarterly content plan in one platform.
  • You want direct social publishing working today, including to LinkedIn (a platform Belken Studio's pending approval list does not include yet).
  • You want a performance dashboard tracking how every post does across every platform from one view.

// Questions

Straight answers

What is the main difference between Belken Studio and Munch Studio?
Munch Studio is a social content management platform: it trains on your brand, plans your content calendar, generates multiple post formats (video, carousels, posters, scripts), and publishes them to your social accounts on schedule. It is built around the problem of maintaining a consistent social presence without doing the planning yourself. Belken Studio is a video-specific tool: it clips long-form video into short captioned segments and generates AI avatar talking-head ads from a script. It does not manage your social calendar or produce non-video content. The fundamental difference is scope: Munch Studio is a full social manager; Belken Studio is a video production tool.
Does Munch Studio clip long videos into short clips?
Yes. Munch Studio includes video repurposing: the Essential plan includes 500 minutes of source video per month and 10 generated videos, and the Premium plan includes 1,000 minutes and 30 generated videos per month. Belken Studio also clips long-form video as its core workflow. The difference is that in Munch Studio, clipping is one feature inside a larger social management platform with content strategy, calendaring, and multi-format generation. In Belken Studio, clipping is the primary job and the tool is optimized specifically for it.
Does Belken Studio publish directly to social media?
Not yet. As of 2026-10-18, studio.belken.ai states that direct publishing to TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, and Facebook is waiting on each platform's API approval. Until that clears, you download the finished clips from Belken Studio and post them from your own accounts. Munch Studio already publishes directly to Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, YouTube, and TikTok as of 2026-10-18.
Does Munch Studio have a free plan?
No free plan is listed on munchstudio.com/pricing as of 2026-10-18. Munch Studio offers a 7-day free trial. The lowest paid plan is Essential at $38 per month billed annually. Belken Studio has a free tier at $0 per month (no card required) that includes 60 minutes of source video per month and downloads of captioned clips, with no AI avatar generation on the free plan.
Which tool is better for AI avatar talking-head ads?
Belken Studio is specifically optimized for AI avatar ad generation. Paste a script and it renders a short-form video with a consistent locked AI avatar, captions burned in, and vertical formatting for TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Reels. The AI ads plan at $49/mo produces roughly 4 to 6 short avatar ads per month (2 generated minutes). The Multiple brands plan at $149/mo produces 12 to 18 ads per month (6 generated minutes). Munch Studio also generates video content — 10 or 30 videos per month depending on plan — but its positioning is social content management, not a dedicated avatar ad tool. If your primary goal is producing AI talking-head ads at volume, Belken Studio is the more focused option.
How much does Munch Studio cost?
Munch Studio has two plans billed annually. The Essential plan is $38 per month ($456 per year) and includes 5 video scripts per week, 100 GB storage, 500 minutes of video repurposing per month, and 10 generated videos per month. The Premium plan is $60 per month ($720 per year) and includes unlimited video scripts, 1 TB storage, 1,000 minutes of video repurposing per month, 30 generated videos per month, unlimited team members, and priority support. Both plans include Business Deep Learning (AI trained on your brand), direct social publishing to 5 platforms, content planner, unlimited posters and carousels, and a performance dashboard. No free plan; 7-day free trial available. Prices from munchstudio.com/pricing, checked 2026-10-18. Prices change.
