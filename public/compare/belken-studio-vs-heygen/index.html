// In plain English

Different tools for different goals

Both can generate a video of an AI presenter speaking from a script. After that, they go different directions.

HeyGen is a broad professional video creation platform. It lets you make AI presenter videos from a script, translate existing videos into other languages using lip-sync AI, and produce content for training, sales, marketing, and presentations. It has a free tier, clear published pricing, and is trusted by 100,000+ businesses.

Belken Studio is narrower by design. It turns a script into a short-form social video with a consistent AI avatar, captions burned in, and vertical formatting for TikTok, Reels, and Shorts. It also clips your own long videos. Then it posts directly to your connected TikTok or YouTube account. The workflow is built around one goal: get short-form content onto your social channels without switching between multiple tools.

If you need video translation, multiple avatar options, enterprise team features, or production across video types beyond social shorts, HeyGen is built for that. If you want a single tool that handles script-to-short, long-video clipping, and social publishing, that is what Belken Studio is designed to do.

// Questions

Straight answers

What is the main difference between Belken Studio and HeyGen?
HeyGen is a professional AI video platform, enterprise scale, video translation into multiple languages with lip-sync AI, multiple avatar types, and use cases from training content to marketing. Belken Studio is built specifically for short-form social video: paste a script and it renders a short with a consistent AI avatar and captions, and it also clips your own long footage into shorts and publishes directly to TikTok and YouTube. If you need multi-language video translation or professional video production across many formats, HeyGen is the larger tool. If you want to create and publish short-form social video, starting from either a script or long footage, without switching between tools, Belken Studio puts it in one place.
Does HeyGen clip long videos into short clips for social media?
Not as a primary feature on the plans we reviewed as of 2026-10-16. HeyGen's main capability is creating new AI presenter videos from a script or translating existing videos into other languages. Belken Studio includes a built-in long-video clipper: import a long video and it extracts short clips with burned-in captions ready for TikTok and YouTube Shorts.
Can both create AI talking head videos from a script?
Yes, both tools can generate a video of an AI avatar speaking text you provide. HeyGen offers multiple avatar types including interactive avatars, photo avatars, and custom avatar cloning on higher plans. Belken Studio uses one locked avatar per account, which means your whole social feed looks like the same consistent creator rather than switching between different faces.
Does Belken Studio support video translation like HeyGen?
Belken Studio's public pages do not list video translation as a feature as of 2026-10-16. HeyGen lists lip-sync translation as one of its headline capabilities, allowing you to take an existing video and re-lip-sync it to a different language automatically. If translating existing videos into other languages is your primary goal, HeyGen is built for that.
How much does each one cost?
HeyGen publishes its pricing: Free at $0 for up to 3 videos per month with no credit card required, Creator at $29 per month, Pro starting at $49 per month, and Business at $149 per month plus $20 per additional seat, as shown on heygen.com/pricing on 2026-10-16. All paid HeyGen plans use credits that vary by video length, AI model used, and complexity. Prices change, so check their page. Belken Studio does not publish a price on its public pages. Pricing is shown in the app when you create an account at studio.belken.ai.
Which should I choose?
If you need professional AI presenter videos across many formats, video translation to multiple languages, multiple avatar options, or enterprise team features, choose HeyGen. If you want a single tool to create short-form social video from a script or from long footage, with direct publishing to TikTok and YouTube and one consistent AI avatar across your feed, choose Belken Studio.
