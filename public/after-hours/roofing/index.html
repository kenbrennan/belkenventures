// How it works

Three things happen while you are off the clock.

01

It answers

Every call your team cannot get to, nights, weekends, or mid-job, gets picked up in your shop's name. No voicemail. No dead air. No caller left hanging.

02

It gets the details

What is wrong, the address, and the best number to call back. The urgent ones get flagged, so a real emergency never looks like a routine message.

03

Your team calls back

You get the call and the details, right away for the ones marked urgent, in the morning for the rest. Your crew does what it does best and books the job.

// Questions

Straight answers.

What does it cost?
We price it per shop after a short setup call. There is no per-minute meter and no long contract. Book a 15-minute setup call and we scope it to your hours and how many after-hours calls you actually get.
Does it work with my existing number?
Yes. You keep your number. We answer the calls you cannot get to, after hours or when the crew is slammed, and leave the rest to you. Nothing about your current phone changes.
What happens on a real emergency?
You tell us what counts as urgent for your roofing company. Those calls get flagged so your on-call person can act on them right away instead of finding out in the morning.
Is the voice a real person?
No. It is an AI voice. The demo line is a fictional shop so you can call it and hear exactly how it handles a call without bothering a real business. Play the customer and see for yourself.
How fast can it be running for my shop?
It is a short setup, not a drawn-out project. We learn your name, your hours, your service area, and what counts as urgent on one call, then we wire it up and run it. We scope the timing with you on the setup call.
