What an AI receptionist does for a dental office

A dental front desk handles more call volume than almost any other local service business. New patient inquiries, existing patient appointments, cancellations, emergency calls, insurance questions; the phone rings constantly, and every unanswered call is a patient who either leaves a voicemail or calls the next practice in their search results.

Belken's AI Receptionist answers every call in your practice's name. It handles the intake conversation (greeting the patient, finding out why they are calling, collecting what it needs to book, and confirming the appointment) without a staff member picking up the phone. After hours, on holidays, during busy patient blocks: every call is answered.

ANSWER

Every call answered in your name

The AI greets callers as your practice, handles the reason for the call, and keeps the conversation moving toward a booking. Your existing phone number is unchanged, callers reach the same number they always have.

INTAKE

Collects what your front desk would collect

For new patients: name, appointment type (new patient exam, cleaning, toothache, cosmetic consultation), insurance carrier, and preferred time. For existing patients: reason for the visit and scheduling preference. No generic script; the AI is configured for how your practice runs.

BOOK

Appointment confirmed to your calendar

The AI books directly to your scheduling system. The caller hangs up with a confirmed appointment time, not a message that someone will call them back to find a slot.

FOLLOW UP

Missed callers get a text in ~60 seconds

Any caller who does not get through receives an automatic text. The text picks up the conversation and can move toward a confirmed booking, no callback from your front desk needed.

REACTIVATE

Inactive patients get a recall call

Patients overdue for a cleaning or checkup get an outbound call by name. The AI re-introduces the recall reminder, answers basic questions, and books the appointment with anyone ready to come in, without any staff time.

New patient intake calls

New patient acquisition is where most dental practices lose the most money in missed calls. A new patient who calls after seeing your Google listing at 8 PM is not going to leave a voicemail and wait until 9 AM to hear back. They call the next practice. If that practice answers, you just lost a patient worth thousands of dollars in lifetime value.

Belken's AI Receptionist answers new patient calls regardless of when they come in. It collects the information your front desk would collect (name, reason for the visit, insurance carrier, and scheduling preference) and books the first available appointment directly to your calendar. The new patient hangs up confirmed, not pending.

New patients booked, not messaged

The AI books the appointment and confirms the time with the caller before hanging up. When your front desk arrives in the morning, the new patient is already on the schedule, not in a callback queue.

After-hours and emergency calls

Dental emergencies do not follow office hours. A patient with a toothache at 10 PM, a broken tooth on a Saturday morning, or a lost crown before a Monday flight needs to reach someone, and if your practice does not answer, they call an emergency dental service or the next practice with Sunday hours.

Belken's AI Receptionist answers 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. When an after-hours patient calls with an urgent issue, the AI captures their name, contact number, and the nature of the emergency. For calls you designate as urgent, it can transfer to an on-call line. For non-urgent after-hours requests (a patient who wants to book first thing Monday) it texts them back and books the earliest available appointment so they are first on the schedule when you open.

The AI knows the difference between a caller who says "my tooth has been hurting for a week and I want to come in" and one who says "I knocked out a tooth, what do I do." The response and escalation path are configured for how your practice handles those situations.

Patient recall and reactivation

Most dental practices have a significant pool of inactive patients, people who came in once or twice and then stopped scheduling. Some moved. Some forgot. Many simply never got a follow-up call that turned into a booked appointment. That inactive list is a filled schedule waiting to happen.

Belken includes patient reactivation in the AI Receptionist. You provide a list of patients who are overdue for a recall or who haven't been in 6, 12, or 18 months. The AI calls each patient by name, references that they are due for their cleaning or checkup, answers basic questions about scheduling and insurance, and books the appointment with anyone ready to come back.

For a dental practice with 400 inactive patients, a 5% reactivation rate from an AI outbound campaign means 20 filled chairs, from a list you already have, without advertising spend.

No staff time on recall calls

Recall calls are the task front desk staff are most likely to deprioritize when the office is busy. The AI runs the campaign in the background (calling patients, handling the conversation, and booking the ones who respond) while your team handles the patients already in the office.
