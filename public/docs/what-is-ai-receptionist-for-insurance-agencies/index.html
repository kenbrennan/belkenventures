What an AI receptionist does for an insurance agency
Insurance agencies run on inbound calls. Every unanswered quote request, every after-hours claims question, and every lapsed-policy reconnection that goes to voicemail is revenue walking out the door.

Answers every call, 24 hours a day

The AI answers in your agency's name around the clock, including evenings, weekends, and holidays. It greets callers professionally, identifies whether they are a new prospect looking for a quote or an existing policyholder with a question, and routes the conversation accordingly.

Handles quote requests from new prospects

For inbound quote calls, the AI collects the coverage type (home, auto, commercial, life, umbrella), property or vehicle details, the caller's current carrier, and their preferred consultation time. It books the appointment directly to your agent's calendar so the prospect hangs up with a confirmed time, not a number to call back.

Texts missed callers back in 60 seconds

When a caller cannot get through, the AI texts them back automatically in about 60 seconds. Insurance shoppers typically call multiple agencies before committing; the first one to respond almost always earns the consultation. Automatic text-back closes that gap before the prospect moves to the next agent on their list.

Routes after-hours claims and urgent calls

For policyholders calling after hours about an accident, storm damage, or emergency loss, the AI captures the claim details and routes urgent situations to an on-call number you designate. Non-urgent questions are logged and scheduled for the next business day so your agents start each morning with a complete call queue, not a pile of voicemails.

Reactivates lapsed policyholders and former clients

Belken's AI Receptionist includes outbound dead-list reactivation. You provide a list of former clients whose policies cancelled, lapsed, or moved to another carrier. The AI calls each person by name, references their previous coverage, and offers a no-obligation rate review. A single reactivated household generating $1,200 per year in premiums pays for a meaningful percentage of the retainer.

Why insurance agencies miss more calls than they think
Independent insurance agents wear multiple hats. When you are in a client meeting, on the phone with an underwriter, or simply at lunch, every inbound call is an unanswered opportunity.

A prospect shopping for homeowner's insurance or an auto bundle will call two or three agencies before deciding where to get a quote. If your agency does not answer, or takes hours to return a voicemail, that prospect is already sitting across from someone else. The speed-to-lead window in insurance is measured in minutes, not hours.

After-hours calls present a different problem. A policyholder who just had a fender bender at 8 PM on a Friday does not want to leave a voicemail. They want to know someone heard them and that the claims process has started. An AI that captures the details, confirms it will be logged, and offers to schedule a follow-up call with an agent gives them that reassurance without pulling your on-call agent into a low-stakes notification call.

Lapsed client reactivation is the lever most agencies leave untouched. Every agency has former policyholders who left for a competitor, let a policy lapse, or simply changed circumstances. Many of them would come back if someone called and offered a rate review. The AI does that systematically, at scale, without your producers spending their selling time on outbound cold calls.
