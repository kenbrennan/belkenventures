What an AI receptionist does for an accounting firm

An AI receptionist answers your accounting firm's inbound calls around the clock. It greets the caller in your firm's name, determines what they need, and books the appointment or consultation directly to your calendar. For calls that come in after hours, during a busy return window, or when staff are all on deadline work, it texts the caller back automatically in about 60 seconds so no lead goes cold.

Belken's AI Receptionist is configured for accounting and CPA practices. It understands the difference between a new client inquiry, an existing client question about a deadline, and a status check on a filed return. It routes each type of call appropriately and books consultations without requiring a human to pick up first.

ANSWER

Picks up in your firm's name

The AI answers as your practice, not a generic menu. It is scripted for accounting services and tuned for the language your clients actually use when they call.

QUALIFY

Determines what the caller needs

New client inquiry, existing client question, tax prep appointment, or deadline-related question. The AI asks the right intake questions and routes accordingly.

BOOK

Consultation lands on your calendar

The AI books directly into whatever scheduling system the firm uses. The caller hears a confirmed time before they hang up, rather than waiting for a callback.

FOLLOW UP

Missed callers get a text in about 60 seconds

Any caller who does not get through receives an automatic text. The conversation can continue by text and convert to a booking without requiring a callback from the firm.

REACTIVATE

Calls lapsed clients to rebook

Past clients who have not engaged in one or more years can be contacted by name and rebooked. Most did not leave intentionally. A direct outreach by name converts them back at high rates.

Handling the tax season call surge

For accounting firms and CPA practices, the phone volume from January through April 15th, and again in late September and October for extension filers, is often three to five times higher than the rest of the year. Those are also the weeks when your staff is most stretched and least available to answer the phone.

An AI receptionist handles every call simultaneously. There is no busy signal, no voicemail during a deadline week, and no prospective client who dials a competitor because they could not get through. Every call either gets booked or escalated, regardless of how many come in at once.

Deadline-week surge is covered at no extra cost

The AI Receptionist handles any volume as part of the flat retainer. There is no per-minute meter that spikes during busy season. High-volume weeks in January, April, and October cost the same as a quiet August week.

The practical impact is that accounting firms with an AI receptionist book the new client consultations that arrive during tax season, rather than missing them. A prospect who calls a firm in February and reaches voicemail will typically call the next firm on the list. An AI receptionist means the consultation gets booked during the first call.

New client intake during busy season

New client acquisition calls are among the highest-value inbound calls an accounting firm receives. They are also the most likely to be missed during tax season when staff are focused on returns. A potential new client calling in February represents several hundred to several thousand dollars of annual recurring revenue, depending on the engagement type.

Belken's AI Receptionist handles new client intake calls at any hour. It asks the questions your firm uses to qualify a new client, including service type, prior firm, complexity of the return or engagement, and timeline. It books the initial consultation on the spot and hands off a complete intake record to the appropriate person at the firm.

The window during which a prospect is actively deciding between accounting firms is short. A firm that answers immediately and books the consultation during the first call wins a disproportionate share of new business compared to a firm that calls back the next day.

After-hours calls from accounting clients

Accounting clients call at odd hours more than clients in most other service businesses. A business owner who realizes on a Sunday night that they have a question before a Monday filing is going to call. A client who receives a notice from the IRS is going to call immediately, not during business hours.

An AI receptionist answers 24 hours a day, including evenings, weekends, and the days before major deadlines. For after-hours calls where no staff member needs to be involved, it books the appointment for the next available slot and texts a confirmation to the client. For genuinely urgent situations, the escalation logic can be configured to route certain call types to a partner's or manager's cell.

Calling lapsed accounting clients to rebook

Most accounting firms have a list of clients who have not engaged in a tax year or more. Some switched firms. Many simply lost touch, forgot to refile after a life event, or had a relationship with a staff member who left. The majority of those lapsed clients did not have a negative experience; they just drifted.

Belken includes a dead-list reactivation feature as part of the AI Receptionist. You upload your list of lapsed clients. The AI calls each one by name, identifies itself as calling from your firm, notes that the current tax year is coming up, and offers to book a consultation. Clients who are ready to re-engage get booked on the spot. This is revenue your firm already paid to earn, sitting in a contact list waiting to be called.
