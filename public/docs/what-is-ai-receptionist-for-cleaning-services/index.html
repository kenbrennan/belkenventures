What an AI receptionist does for a cleaning company

A cleaning business runs on recurring appointments. Each new customer who calls and gets an answer is worth hundreds of dollars a month in recurring revenue. Each one who calls and hits voicemail is gone — they call the next company on the list.

An AI receptionist answers every inbound call in your business name, 24 hours a day. It asks for the caller's address, preferred schedule, and what type of service they need. It books the appointment directly to your existing calendar. If the caller cannot stay on the line, it sends a text within about 60 seconds so they know you saw the call.

Beyond answering calls, Belken's AI Receptionist also makes outbound calls to fill gaps in your schedule — either from your waitlist when a cancellation opens a slot, or from your list of past customers who have not scheduled in a while.

How it works — 5 steps

Step 1

Call comes in any time

A potential customer calls your business number. The AI answers immediately in your business name — day, night, or weekend. The caller hears a professional greeting, not a voicemail.

Step 2

Qualify and collect information

The AI asks for the caller's name, address, preferred cleaning frequency (weekly, biweekly, monthly, or one-time), and any special requests. It explains your service options naturally in conversation.

Step 3

Book the appointment

The AI checks your calendar and books the appointment directly. The customer gets a confirmation. You see the new booking without picking up the phone or logging into a system.

Step 4

Missed-call text-back within 60 seconds

If a caller hangs up before the AI can help them, or if there is a connection issue, the AI sends a text message within about 60 seconds. Most callers respond. You do not lose the lead to silence.

Step 5

Outbound: fill slots and reactivate lapsed customers

When a cancellation opens, the AI calls your waitlist to fill it. On a schedule you set, it also calls past customers who have not booked in a while — greeting them by name and offering to rebook. No manual dialing required.

Filling cancellation slots

Last-minute cancellations are one of the biggest scheduling headaches for cleaning companies. A crew that shows up for four jobs but only has three — because one customer cancelled that morning — represents a direct loss on labor costs.

Belken's AI Receptionist handles this automatically. When a slot opens, the AI calls the next customer on your waitlist by name, explains the available opening, and rebooks it. You do not need to make calls yourself or ask your team to do it.

The math for recurring customers

A standard biweekly residential cleaning runs $100 to $300 per visit. A customer who books 26 times a year is worth $2,600 to $7,800 annually. Filling one cancellation slot per week from the waitlist adds $5,000 to $15,000 in annual revenue that would otherwise go to a competitor.

Reactivating lapsed customers

Most cleaning companies have a list of past customers who booked once or twice, then stopped. They did not have a bad experience — they just got busy, moved, or forgot. Calling that list manually never happens because the owner has no time.

Belken's AI Receptionist calls those customers by name, reminds them your business is ready, and rebooks the ones who are ready to start again. You upload the list. The AI handles the calls. Reactivated customers are often the easiest closes because they already trust you.

  • Upload your old customer list in a spreadsheet
  • The AI calls each customer by name and references their previous service
  • Customers who are ready get rebooked immediately to your calendar
  • No manual dialing, no staff time, no awkward "remember us?" outreach

After-hours and weekend coverage

People search for and call cleaning services at all hours — especially when they are preparing for a move-out inspection, a vacation rental turnover, or an event. A cleaning company that answers at 9 PM books the job. One that sends the caller to voicemail does not.

The AI Receptionist answers every call 24/7, including evenings, weekends, and holidays. There is no after-hours charge and no per-minute meter. The flat retainer covers all calls regardless of volume or time of day.
