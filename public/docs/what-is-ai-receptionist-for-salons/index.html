What it does for your salon
Hair salons and barbershops field the same calls all day — new client inquiries, appointment requests, reschedules, and last-minute same-day slots. The AI handles every one of them without putting anyone on hold or sending a caller to voicemail.

Answers every call in your salon's name while stylists are with clients

When your stylists are all booked and the front desk can't pick up, the AI answers immediately in your salon's name. Callers get a real conversation, not "please leave a message." It handles new client inquiries, returning client bookings, service questions, and pricing calls the same way a trained receptionist would.

Books appointments with the right stylist at the requested time

When a caller asks for a balayage with their regular stylist on Saturday afternoon, the AI checks availability and books the slot. If the preferred stylist is full, it offers the next available time or an alternative. They hang up with a confirmed appointment — not a callback promise that gets lost between clients.

Texts missed callers back in about 60 seconds

When someone calls and can't get through — the salon is slammed, it's after hours, or the call came in during a color application — the AI texts them back automatically within a minute. The text opens a conversation that can move directly to a booked appointment. Salon clients who call two or three places tend to book the first one that responds, and voicemail doesn't count.

Takes after-hours calls and books next-day appointments

A significant share of salon calls happen in the evening — clients finishing work, planning their weekend, or remembering they need a trim before an event. The AI answers at 9 PM the same as 9 AM, captures their preferred service and time, and books them on the schedule before they fall asleep and forget to call back.

Calls dormant clients to rebook them before they find a different salon

You have clients who came in three or four times, then disappeared. The AI calls each one by name, references their last visit and the service they had, and offers to get them back on the schedule. For salons where a loyal client is worth $800 to $2,000 or more per year in recurring business, reactivating even a fraction of a lapsed list moves the revenue needle significantly.

AI receptionist vs. Vagaro and GlossGenius online booking
Online booking captures clients who are already on your website. The AI captures clients who pick up the phone.

Tools like Vagaro, GlossGenius, Boulevard, and Square Appointments handle clients who are motivated enough to visit your booking page and fill out a form. Those tools work well for their use case. If those calls go to voicemail, no online booking system catches them. The AI Receptionist and your existing booking software work in parallel, not as replacements for each other.
