What an AI receptionist does for a chiropractic practice

A chiropractic office runs on phone calls. New patients call to book a first evaluation after a car accident or a flare-up of back pain. Existing patients call to schedule their next adjustment, reschedule when something comes up, or ask whether you take their insurance. Patients who came in a few times and got relief call months later when the pain returns.

The problem is that your front desk staff are often unavailable when these calls come in — because they are rooming a patient, collecting payment, verifying benefits, or just away from the desk. A missed call from a prospective patient searching for a chiropractor is a patient who immediately calls the next result in their search.

Belken's AI Receptionist is configured for chiropractic practices. It answers every call in your practice's name, handles new patient intake end to end, books appointments directly to your calendar, and texts any missed caller back in about 60 seconds.

How new patient intake works

When a new patient calls, the AI collects what your front desk would gather before booking:

  • Patient name and best callback number
  • Chief complaint — back pain, neck pain, sciatica, headaches, car accident injury, sports injury, or general wellness
  • Insurance carrier and plan, if the patient has one
  • Preferred appointment day and time

The AI then books the new patient evaluation directly onto your schedule. The patient hangs up with a confirmed appointment — not a voicemail that will be returned whenever someone has a moment.

Existing patients calling to reschedule or book a follow-up adjustment get the same treatment: the AI finds an open slot that works, books it, and confirms the time before hanging up.

After-hours calls for chiropractic offices

Chiropractic practices often see after-hours call volume for two reasons. First, patients want to book their next adjustment the same evening it is due — after a long day at a desk, they feel the tension returning and they want to get on the schedule before it gets worse. Second, car accidents, sports injuries, and acute back episodes happen on weekends and evenings when the office is closed.

The AI Receptionist answers all of these calls. For a caller who needs to book a follow-up, it finds the next available slot and confirms the appointment. For a caller describing an acute injury, it captures their name, contact information, and a description of what happened, then books the earliest available appointment so they are first on the schedule the next morning. For situations requiring immediate clinical attention, it can transfer the call to an on-call number you designate.

Missed-call text-back for chiropractic offices

When a caller does not get through — because your front desk is with a patient, the line is busy, or it is after hours — the AI sends a text message automatically in about 60 seconds. The message is conversational and can move toward a confirmed booking without requiring the caller to call back.

For a chiropractic office, the speed matters. Someone searching for a chiropractor after an injury or a bad morning with their back is going to call two or three practices in sequence. The first one to respond gets the appointment. A 60-second text beats a two-hour voicemail callback every time.

Reactivating patients who stopped coming

Most chiropractic practices have a large list of past patients who came in for acute care, got enough relief to function, and stopped coming before completing their care plan. These patients did not switch providers. They just stopped calling.

Belken includes patient reactivation in the AI Receptionist. You provide a list of past patients who are overdue — patients who last came in 3, 6, or 12 months ago and have not rebooked. The AI calls each patient by name, references that they are due for a follow-up or that completing their original care plan will help prevent the pain from returning, and books the appointment with anyone who is ready to come back.

For chiropractic practices, reactivation converts past acute patients into ongoing maintenance patients. That is recurring revenue from a relationship you already built, without any staff time or new patient acquisition cost.
