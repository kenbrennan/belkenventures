What an AI receptionist does for a law firm

An AI receptionist answers your law firm's inbound calls around the clock. It greets callers in your firm's name, collects intake information, and either books the consultation directly to your calendar or routes a time-sensitive legal matter to the on-call attorney's cell phone.

Belken's AI Receptionist is configured for legal practices. It understands the difference between a caller who wants to schedule an estate planning consultation next week and a caller whose spouse just had them served with emergency custody papers, and it handles each one differently. For calls that come in when nobody answers, it texts the caller back automatically in about 60 seconds so the potential client does not contact a competing firm while they are still deciding where to go.

ANSWER

Picks up in your firm's name

The AI answers as your firm, not a generic menu. It is scripted for legal intake and tuned for how your calls actually sound, new clients, existing client questions, consultation requests, and after-hours urgent matters.

QUALIFY

Gathers what it needs to book or escalate

Caller's name, contact details, practice area, brief description of the matter, and urgency level. The AI distinguishes a routine consultation request from a time-sensitive legal situation before deciding the next step.

BOOK

Consultation appointment goes on your calendar

Routine consultations get booked directly into whatever scheduling tool the firm already uses. The caller hears a confirmed appointment time before they hang up, and a complete intake record lands in your system.

ESCALATE

Urgent matters go to a real attorney

DUI arrest, emergency custody, domestic violence situation, restraining order; the AI routes the call live to the on-call attorney's cell phone. Time-sensitive legal matters do not go to a booking queue.

FOLLOW UP

Missed callers get a text in ~60 seconds

Any caller who does not get through receives an automatic text. The conversation continues by text and can convert to a booked consultation without a callback, before the caller finds another attorney.

How urgent legal calls are handled after hours

Many of the most valuable legal matters arrive outside business hours. A DUI arrest happens at 2 AM on a Saturday. An emergency protective order needs to be filed before a court opens Monday morning. A business partner locks someone out of their own company on a holiday weekend. These callers are not browsing, they are in crisis, they are calling the first number they can find, and they will hire the first attorney who picks up.

Belken's AI Receptionist is configured to recognize when a caller is describing a time-sensitive matter. When that happens, it routes the call live to the on-call attorney's cell phone. It does not send a time-sensitive matter to a voicemail or a consultation booking flow. The escalation logic (exactly which matter types and phrases trigger a live transfer) is configured during setup to match your firm's practice areas and on-call coverage.

After-hours coverage built into the flat rate

The AI Receptionist runs 24/7 as part of the retainer. There is no separate after-hours add-on. Urgent legal matters happen at night and on weekends, that coverage is included at the flat rate, not billed per-minute when you need it most.

New client intake: what gets collected on every call

The AI Receptionist collects complete intake information on every new caller before the consultation is booked. The attorney or intake coordinator sees a full record, not a message slip with a name and a phone number.

For a new client call, the AI gathers:

  • Full name and contact information (phone, email)
  • Practice area, criminal, family, personal injury, estate, business, etc.
  • Brief description of the matter in the caller's own words
  • Urgency level and any relevant dates (court dates, filing deadlines)
  • How they found the firm (referral, search, prior client)
  • Preferred consultation format and available times

That record goes directly into your intake system or CRM. Every consultation is pre-loaded with context before the attorney walks in. No intake call to return. No duplicate questions when the client arrives.

Consultation booking: free, paid, and matter-specific

Not all consultations are the same. A criminal defense firm might offer a free 30-minute initial consultation. An estate planning practice might charge for a 90-minute intake meeting. A business law firm might route different matter types (contract disputes, business formation, employment) to different attorneys with different calendar availability.

Belken configures the AI Receptionist to handle these distinctions correctly. Free initial consultations, paid consultations, and matter-type-specific appointments each route to the right calendar and the right attorney. The caller hears a confirmed appointment before they hang up. No follow-up call needed to finalize the booking.

After-hours consultations can be booked for the next available morning or afternoon slot. The caller does not have to wait on hold or call back during business hours to get on the schedule.

Rebook former clients

Most law firms have a database of former clients whose matters closed but whose legal needs did not end. The estate planning client who executed their will three years ago and has not called since buying a new house. The business client whose LLC formation closed but who has since taken on partners. The family law client whose divorce finalized but who needs to modify a custody agreement.

Belken includes former-client reactivation as part of the AI Receptionist. You upload a list of past clients. The AI calls each one by name, mentions that your firm is available for new matters, and books a consultation for anyone who is ready. This is revenue your firm already paid to earn, it is sitting in a closed-file database waiting to be called.

Reactivation calls go out automatically on a cadence Belken configures during setup. You see which former clients booked, which declined, and which asked to be called back at a different time. No paralegal time spent on outreach calls.
