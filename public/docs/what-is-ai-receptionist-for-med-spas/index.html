What an AI receptionist does for a med spa

A med spa has a fundamental phone problem: your front desk is often with a client during a treatment, which is exactly when inbound booking calls come in. A prospective Botox client who cannot reach you does not leave a voicemail. They try the next spa in their search results. That is a high-margin appointment, already lost.

Belken's AI Receptionist answers every call in your spa's name, around the clock. It books consultations and treatments directly to your calendar, texts missed callers back in about 60 seconds, and calls your past clients by name when they are due for their next treatment. Belken sets it up, monitors it, and operates it — your front desk does not touch any of this.

ANSWER

Every call answered in your spa's name

When your front desk is in a treatment room, on another call, or closed for the evening, your AI receptionist picks up. It greets callers in your spa's name, handles booking requests, and keeps the conversation moving toward a confirmed appointment.

BOOK

Appointment confirmed to your calendar

The AI books directly to your scheduling system. The caller hangs up with a confirmed time for their consultation, Botox appointment, filler session, or facial — not a message that someone will call them back.

TEXT BACK

Missed callers get a text in ~60 seconds

Any caller who does not get through receives a conversational text automatically in about 60 seconds. The text can move toward a confirmed booking, so no missed call becomes a lost appointment.

CONFIRM

Appointments confirmed to cut no-shows

Your AI receptionist confirms every appointment after booking. An empty treatment chair is direct revenue lost and a slot that is almost impossible to fill last-minute. Confirmed appointments with reminders cut the no-show rate without any manual follow-up from your team.

REACTIVATE

Past clients called on their treatment cycle

Botox fades. Filler softens. Facials are a monthly routine. Your clients are due — they just forgot. The AI calls each past client by name to rebook them on their natural cycle. Your highest-margin appointments, filled without advertising spend.

Calls during treatments

The busiest booking window for a med spa is the after-work rush — roughly 5 PM to 8 PM — which overlaps with the tail end of your treatment schedule and happens to be when new clients are searching on their phones. Your front desk is slammed. The phones ring. Calls go to voicemail.

Belken's AI Receptionist runs in parallel with your front desk. While your team is finishing appointments, the AI is answering every call that comes in, booking the ones that want to schedule, and texting back the ones that disconnect before speaking with anyone. Your front desk walks out of the last treatment to a fully booked calendar, not a voicemail queue.

You keep your existing phone number

Your spa keeps the number clients already know. The AI picks up when your front desk cannot, mid-treatment, after hours, or all the time. Nothing changes for your clients.

After-hours booking requests

Treatments get researched late in the evening. A prospective client reads about Botox, filler, or a facial at 10 PM and wants to book before they forget. If your spa goes to voicemail at that moment, the appointment does not happen — it gets lost to sleep, to distraction, or to a competitor who does answer.

Your AI receptionist answers after-hours calls in your spa's name, handles questions about services and availability, and books the consultation while other spas ring to voicemail. Any after-hours caller who disconnects without booking gets a text-back in about 60 seconds, with an offer to book through text instead.

For a med spa with six or eight treatment slots per day, capturing two additional after-hours bookings a week is meaningful revenue. The AI handles this automatically, every night, without adding a staff shift.

Treatment cycle reactivation

Most med spas have a large pool of past clients who came in once or twice and then stopped scheduling. Some got busy. Some forgot the name of the spa when it was time to rebook. Many never got a follow-up call that turned into an appointment. That dormant list is the highest-return marketing asset you have.

Belken includes dead-list reactivation in the AI Receptionist. You upload your client list. The AI calls each past client by name, acknowledges that it has been a while, references their treatment history (Botox is typically every three to four months, filler every six to twelve, facials as often as monthly), and books the appointment with anyone ready to come back.

These are the easiest closes in your business. The client already knows your spa, has already trusted you with a treatment, and has an obvious reason to return. They just need someone to call.

No staff time on reactivation calls

Outbound calls to past clients are the task front desk staff are most likely to skip when the spa is busy. The AI runs the campaign in the background — calling clients, handling the conversation, booking the ones who are ready — while your team focuses on clients already in the spa.

Reducing no-shows

A no-show at a med spa is particularly costly. Treatment slots are time-blocked, equipment is prepared, and the opening in the chair is almost impossible to fill at the last minute. A 20% no-show rate on a six-slot day is more than a missed booking; it is direct overhead spent on nothing.

Your AI receptionist confirms every appointment immediately after booking, then sends a reminder before the scheduled time. Clients who confirm are far less likely to no-show. Clients who need to reschedule are caught before the slot is wasted, giving you time to fill it from a waitlist or from an outbound reactivation call.

Frequently asked questions

Does an AI receptionist work with my existing scheduling system?

Belken configures the AI to work with how your spa books appointments today. During the 20-minute setup call, Belken asks which system you use and how you want bookings handled. You do not need to switch software.

Will clients know they are talking to an AI?

The AI is transparent about what it is when asked directly. In practice, clients who call to book a treatment care about getting their appointment confirmed quickly, not about whether the call is handled by a person or an AI. The AI is configured to sound natural and helpful for med spa conversations.

What happens to calls that need a human?

The AI can transfer calls to a designated number for situations that require a human — complex medical questions, escalations, or anything the AI is configured to pass through. Most booking and intake conversations do not require a transfer.

Can it handle multiple treatment types?

Yes. During setup, Belken configures the AI with your full treatment menu — Botox, filler, facials, body treatments, consultations, whatever you offer. The AI handles booking requests for each service type according to how you want calls managed.
