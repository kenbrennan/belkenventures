What an AI receptionist does for a real estate agent

An AI receptionist answers every inbound call to your real estate business around the clock. It greets the caller in your name, qualifies the inquiry — buyer, seller, investor, or renter — and books a showing, consult call, or discovery appointment directly onto your calendar. For calls that come in when you are in a showing, on another call, or off hours, it texts the caller back automatically in about 60 seconds so new leads do not go cold.

Belken's AI Receptionist is tuned for real estate conversations. It understands the difference between a buyer asking about a specific listing and a seller wanting a market analysis, asks the questions a real estate front desk would ask, and routes urgency appropriately — without putting a caller on hold or sending them to voicemail.

ANSWER

Picks up in your name

The AI answers as your business, not a generic service. It is configured for how real estate inquiries actually sound — listing questions, price reductions, market inquiries, and seller consultations.

QUALIFY

Gathers what it needs to book

Caller's name, what property or service they are interested in, their timeline, and preferred appointment time. Real estate qualification is different from a trades call — the AI knows the difference.

BOOK

Showing or consult hits your calendar

The AI books directly into your Google or Outlook calendar. The caller hears a confirmed time before they hang up — no follow-up call needed to lock it in.

FOLLOW UP

Missed callers get a text in ~60 seconds

Any caller who does not get through receives an automatic text. The conversation continues over text and can move to a confirmed booking without you making a callback.

REACTIVATE

Past clients and old leads get a call

The dead-list reactivation feature calls past clients and stale leads by name, re-introduces your service, and books a call with anyone who is ready to move — buyers who went quiet, past sellers whose neighbors are now listing.

After-hours buyer and seller calls

Real estate leads do not follow business hours. A buyer browsing listings at 9 PM on a Saturday who calls about a price drop is not going to wait until Monday to find out if they can schedule a showing. If you cannot answer, they move to the next agent on the list — and that agent answers.

Belken's AI Receptionist answers 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Evening buyer inquiries, weekend showing requests, early-morning seller questions — every call gets answered and either booked or followed up, the same as a business-hours call. There is no separate after-hours tier and no per-minute charge for night and weekend volume.

For real estate agents who work solo or on small teams, this is the practical difference between capturing a lead and losing it. You do not need to be available every hour — the AI handles the call while you finish the showing, drive between listings, or sleep.

How showing and consult booking works

When a buyer calls about a listing or requests a showing, the AI gathers what it needs to book the appointment: the caller's name, the property address or listing they are interested in, their preferred showing time, and any qualifying details you want captured. It then books the appointment directly onto your calendar.

The same process applies to seller consultation calls. A homeowner thinking about listing who calls at 7 PM gets a confirmed consult appointment for a time that works for both of you — without a round of phone tag to find a slot.

No phone tag to confirm showings

The AI books directly to your calendar and confirms the time with the caller before hanging up. You see the appointment, the caller details, and the property they want to see — nothing to chase down.

Re-engage old leads and past clients

Most real estate agents have a pipeline of leads that went quiet — buyers who were ready to look six months ago and then paused, seller inquiries that never converted, past clients whose neighbors are now listing. That pipeline cost money to build (advertising, referrals, open house follow-ups) and most of it sits in a spreadsheet.

Belken includes a dead-list reactivation feature in the AI Receptionist. You upload your old lead list or past-client database. The AI calls each contact by name, re-introduces your service with a relevant hook (a market update, a new listing in their price range, or a simple "we worked together two years ago and wanted to check in"), and books a call or showing with anyone who is ready to move.

For real estate, the reactivation math is straightforward: one closed transaction from a re-engaged past client pays for months of the retainer.
