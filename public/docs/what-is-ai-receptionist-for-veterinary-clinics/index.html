What an AI receptionist does for a veterinary clinic

An AI receptionist answers your clinic's inbound calls around the clock. It greets callers in your clinic's name, collects patient and appointment information, and either books the visit directly to your schedule or routes a pet emergency to the on-call vet's cell phone.

Belken's AI Receptionist is configured for veterinary practices. It knows the difference between a routine wellness appointment request and a caller describing a dog that just swallowed something toxic. Routine calls get booked on the spot. Emergencies get escalated before the owner has a chance to hang up and call an emergency clinic instead.

ANSWER

Picks up in your clinic's name

The AI answers as your clinic, not a generic automated menu. It is scripted for veterinary intake and tuned for how your calls actually come in: wellness visits, sick-pet calls, prescription refills, appointment reminders, and after-hours emergencies.

TRIAGE

Tells routine from emergency

The AI asks about the pet's symptoms and urgency. A dog who is limping is not the same call as a dog who cannot breathe. Routine appointments go to the booking flow. Emergency symptoms trigger immediate escalation to the on-call vet.

BOOK

Appointment goes on your calendar

Wellness exams, vaccine updates, dental cleanings, sick visits, and surgery consults each book directly into your scheduling system. The caller hears a confirmed appointment time before they hang up, and the full intake record is already in your system.

ESCALATE

Emergencies go to a real vet

Difficulty breathing, seizures, uncontrolled bleeding, suspected poisoning, trauma from an accident, the AI routes the call live to the on-call vet's cell. Pet owners in a genuine emergency do not get a voicemail.

FOLLOW UP

Missed callers get a text in ~60 seconds

Any caller who does not get through receives an automatic text. The conversation continues by text and can convert to a booked appointment, before the owner decides to drive to the emergency clinic across town.

How after-hours pet emergencies are handled

Pet emergencies do not schedule themselves during business hours. A cat that ate something toxic calls for help at 9 PM. A dog hit by a car needs a vet on a Sunday morning. A puppy that has been vomiting for six hours is a crisis at midnight. These callers are not shopping around, they are scared, and they will take their pet wherever the phone gets answered.

Belken's AI Receptionist is configured to recognize emergency symptoms when a caller describes them. When that happens, the call routes live to the on-call vet's cell phone. It does not send an emergency to a voicemail or a booking flow. The specific symptom list and escalation logic is configured during setup to match your clinic's on-call coverage and emergency protocols.

After-hours coverage at no extra charge

The AI Receptionist runs 24/7 as part of the flat retainer. There is no per-call or per-minute charge for after-hours coverage. Emergencies happen at night and on weekends, that coverage is built in at the same rate.

Appointment booking: every type, every slot

Most veterinary scheduling is not complicated: the caller wants a wellness exam, a vaccine booster, or a sick visit for a pet that is not acting right. But getting those appointments booked while the clinic is at full capacity, phones are ringing during check-in, and staff are focused on the patients in the room, that is where the front desk breaks down and callers end up in voicemail.

The AI Receptionist handles the entire booking flow. It asks about the pet, the reason for the visit, and preferred times. It matches the appointment type to the right calendar slot and the right doctor if your clinic has multiple vets with different availability or specialties. The caller gets a confirmed appointment before they hang up.

Different appointment types, wellness, sick visit, dental, surgery consult, vaccine-only, can each route to different calendar durations and different staff. Your scheduling logic stays intact. The AI just fills the slots.

Patient intake: what gets collected before every visit

The AI Receptionist collects a complete patient record on every new caller and a reason-for-visit update on existing patients. Your front-desk team sees a full picture before the appointment, not just a name and a callback number.

For a new patient call, the AI gathers:

  • Owner name, phone number, and email
  • Pet name, species, breed, and age or date of birth
  • Reason for the visit: wellness, sick, vaccine, dental, surgical consult, or other
  • Any current symptoms the owner is observing
  • Preferred veterinarian if the caller has a preference
  • How they found your clinic: referral, search, drive-by, social
  • Preferred appointment date and time

For existing patients, the AI confirms the pet name and reason for the visit before booking. That record lands in your practice management system before the appointment. No intake paperwork to complete at the front desk. No duplicate questions for returning clients.

Annual recall and patient reactivation

Most veterinary practices have a large pool of patients who are overdue for annual wellness exams, vaccine boosters, dental cleanings, or heartworm testing. These are active clients who came in once, twice, or more, and then stopped booking. They did not leave the practice, they just did not get a call that moved them to action.

Belken includes patient reactivation as part of the AI Receptionist. You provide a list of patients with overdue care: animals whose annual wellness exam is past due, dogs whose heartworm test is six months overdue, cats whose dental was recommended a year ago but never scheduled. The AI calls each owner by name, mentions that their pet is due for care, and books the appointment for anyone who is ready.

Reactivation calls go out on a cadence Belken configures. You see which patients booked, which declined, and which asked to be called back at a specific time. This is revenue your clinic already earned the relationship for, it just needs the follow-through call that a busy front desk never gets to make.
